## Why a token at all

ArcticTradeLanes.com is an open, multilingual Arctic GIS OSINT platform (Northern Sea Route,
Northwest Passage, Polar Silk Road; EPSG:3996 polar atlas). The long-term goal ("Project North Star",
see `homepage-spec-20261018.md`) is an entity graph of Arctic ports, lanes, companies, programs and
events, served API-first with alerts. A community token is one possible way to align contributors,
data users and supporters with that goal. It is being explored, not committed to.
