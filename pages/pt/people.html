Pessoas-chave do Ártico

Oradores do megapainel “Arctic Development: Unlocking the Potential of the Northern Sea Route” do AIM Congress 2026 (Dubai, 9 de setembro de 2026). Perfis públicos neutros.

Alexey Chekunkov

Minister for the Development of the Russian Far East and Arctic

Federal ministry responsible for the Russian Far East and the Arctic zone, including Northern Sea Route infrastructure and Arctic ports.

Andrey Chibis

Governor of the Murmansk Region

The Murmansk Region hosts the ice-free port of Murmansk, the western gateway of the Northern Sea Route.

Aisen Nikolaev

Head of the Republic of Sakha (Yakutia)

Sakha (Yakutia) fronts the Laptev and East Siberian seas and hosts the Northern Sea Route port of Tiksi.

Artyom Dovlatov

Deputy Chairman, VEB.RF

VEB.RF is a state development corporation that finances infrastructure projects, including in the Arctic zone.

Vladimir Panov

Special Representative of Rosatom for the Development of the Arctic

Rosatom is the infrastructure operator of the Northern Sea Route and runs the nuclear icebreaker fleet.

Kirill Dmitriev

Chief Executive, Russian Direct Investment Fund (RDIF)

Heads a sovereign co-investment fund; mentioned in the post announcing the AIM Congress 2026 Northern Sea Route panel (not a listed panelist).
